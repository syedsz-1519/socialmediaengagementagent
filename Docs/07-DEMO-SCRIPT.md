# NEXA — 60-Second Hackathon Demo

**Version:** 1.0

---

## Opening — 0–10 seconds

Show dashboard.

Say:

> “Most AI content tools can generate a post. But they don't actually learn what your audience has taught your brand.”

---

## Problem — 10–20 seconds

Show generic recommendation.

Say:

> “Imagine a small business owner who has posted for months. Their account contains hundreds of audience signals, but that knowledge disappears when they ask AI for the next post.”

---

## NEXA — 20–30 seconds

Open Memory.

Say:

> “NEXA turns those interactions into persistent brand memory using Hindsight.”

Show:

- audience insight;
- confidence;
- evidence;
- learning timeline.

---

## Learning — 30–40 seconds

Show changed belief.

Say:

> “Instead of simply remembering posts, NEXA learns patterns from repeated experiences.”

Show:

```text
Generic promotion
↓
weak performance

Educational content
↓
strong saves

Problem-based hooks
↓
strong comments
```

---

## Agent — 40–55 seconds

Enter:

> “Create tomorrow's Instagram Reel.”

Show generated output.

Then open:

> “Why?”

Show memories used.

Say:

> “This recommendation isn't generic. NEXA can show exactly which memories changed its decision.”

---

## Closing — 55–60 seconds

Say:

> **“NEXA doesn't just remember your audience. It learns from them — and makes your next piece of content smarter.”**

---

## Demo Rule

Do not spend the demo explaining implementation before showing the learning effect.

The judge should see:

```text
Problem
→ Memory
→ Learning
→ Better decision
```

within the first minute.
